+ ## Top 3 Favorite Movies
+
+ ### 1. The Intern (2015)
+ - **Director:** Nancy Meyers
+ -	**Genre:** Comedy / Drama
+ 	**Why I Love It:**
+   *The Intern shows that age doesn’t define your purpose.
+   It’s a heartwarming story about friendship, **learning new things**, and **staying positive in every stage of life**.
+
+
+
+
+ ### 2. Forrest Gump (1994)
+  **Director:** Robert Zemeckis
+  **Genre:** Drama / Comedy
+  **Why I Love It:**
+ This movie proves that anyone can achieve great things with kindness, faith, and perseverance.
+ Forrest’s journey reminds me that **staying true to yourself brings happiness** and **success**.
+
+ > *“Life is like a box of chocolates. You never know what you’re gonna get.”* – Forrest Gump
+
+ ---
+
+ ### 3. Encanto (2021)
+ - **Director:** Jared Bush, Byron Howard
+ - **Genre:** Animation / Family / Musical
+ - **Why I Love It:**
+ *Encanto* celebrates family, uniqueness, and self-acceptance. It teaches that even without special powers, you are still **important and loved**. The songs and colors also make it so uplifting.
